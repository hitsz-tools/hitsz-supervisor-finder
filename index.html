  • Each CS supervisor takes at most 6 students, Chinese and international students counted together (计算机学院老师接收国内外学生上限为6).
  • You can only choose supervisors from your own school. Main-campus students on exchange in Shenzhen are not part of this allocation.
  • Before you are admitted you can see your status and change your application. Once a supervisor admits you, the choice is final.
  • If you miss a round, you can only apply in the next one.
  • The topic must match the task book (任务书). The system compares your report and thesis titles with it. In principle the topic and supervisor cannot change after the mid-term check.
  • Students who hope for 推免 are advised to pick the supervisor they want for their master's.

Tips


    

  • Agree before 8 December. The system only records what you and the supervisor already agreed. An explicit "yes, apply to me in the 1st round" is worth more than any ordering trick.
    • 

  • Show work, not a list of skills. A small piece of work on the supervisor's topic (a short implementation or a summary of one of their papers) is the strongest thing you can attach.
    • 

  • Ask how many students have already agreed. With 6 places, popular supervisors are often full after the 1st round.
    • 

  • Pick backups among co-authors. In each profile, "Works with" lists colleagues from this list who publish with them. A co-author of your first choice usually works on related topics and may accept a recommendation.
    • 

  • Promise your 1st choice to one person only. Talking to several supervisors is fine; promising the same round to two of them is not.
    • 

  • Check the topic title. When topics appear (27 Nov – 3 Dec), make sure the title is the one you agreed on, because it must match your task book.
    • 

  • International students: most homepages are in Chinese. Ask in the first email whether you can communicate and write the thesis in English.
    • 




About the data


    

  • Supervisor list: the official list shared by the school (Tencent Docs, 116 rows).
    • 

  • Profiles: each supervisor's page on homepage.hit.edu.cn and the biography text it loads. These are self-descriptions and have not been checked independently.
    • 

  • Research summaries and topic tags are short English summaries written from those pages, not official text.
    • 

  • AI in thesis topics is a forecast for the bachelor graduation topics, since real topics appear only on 27 Nov. Supervisors usually give undergraduates topics from their own research, so the forecast follows it: "Likely AI" when the main field is machine learning, NLP, vision, agents or data mining; "Possibly AI" when AI is a side topic; "Unlikely AI" when it is not mentioned.
    • 

  • English is estimated from signs on the page (English bio, study or work abroad, teaching in English). It has not been tested.
    • 

  • "Mentions undergraduates" means the page invites undergraduates or lists undergraduate papers or awards.
    • 

  • "Works with" counts how often another supervisor's name appears in the publication list. It is approximate.
    • 

  • Views is the homepage counter (人气). It reflects how often a page is opened, not research quality.
    • 

  • Not checked: the external homepages of Nie Liqiang and Fei Hao. Rows with problems in the official list are marked in their profiles.
